About & contact

BioCraftEd Studios is a London bioscientist / game-dev studio. We make synthetic biology more accessible — through a gamified Lab and a separate Games programme — and we develop across web, phone and VR. Availability differs by project; the Lab and Games hubs list what you can open today.
